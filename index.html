Gmail cleanup

Gmail cleanup is a private tool that one person runs on their own computer to manage their own Gmail account. It is not a public service, there is nothing to sign up for, and nobody else's mail is involved.

It looks through the owner's mailbox, labels and archives messages, moves unwanted messages to Trash, reads messages when asked, and sends reports and notices from the owner's account. It cannot permanently delete mail, because it never asks Google for that permission.
