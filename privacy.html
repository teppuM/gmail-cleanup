Gmail cleanup privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026.

Gmail cleanup is a private tool used by one person, its owner, on the owner's own Gmail account. It is not offered to anyone else and does not collect information from the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the tool uses Google's gmail.modify permission. That lets it read messages and their details, change labels, archive messages, move messages to Trash, and send mail from the owner's account. It cannot permanently delete mail.

Where the information goes

The sign-in and anything the tool saves stay on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or given to other people. This website collects nothing itself.

The owner sometimes runs the tool with Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic. When that happens, mail details the tool retrieves can be sent to Anthropic so the assistant can process them for the owner.

How long it is kept

Saved information stays on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it.

Removing access

The owner can end the tool's access at any time at Google account permissions.

Changes and contact

Any change to this policy is posted on this page. Questions can be left on the project's issues page.
